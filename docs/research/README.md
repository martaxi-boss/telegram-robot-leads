# Research Records

Research preserves the investigation performed **before** a material decision is accepted.

## Naming

Prefer: `RQ-xxx-short-topic.md` for scoped research questions. Existing topic-level research may remain when it predates the numbering model and should be linked from future records.

## Required content

- ID/status/date;
- question and context;
- relevant requirements/constraints;
- alternatives considered;
- evaluation criteria;
- evidence and experiments;
- trade-offs/risks;
- conclusion/recommendation;
- confidence level;
- open questions and next action.

Use `../templates/research-question.md`.

Research is not automatically a decision. A material accepted choice should be recorded in `../decisions/`.
